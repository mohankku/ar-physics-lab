Falling object with air drag

Models m·dv/dt = m·g − ½·ρ·Cd·A·v²: gravity pulls the object down, and air pushes back harder the faster it goes, until it reaches its terminal speed. Typical uses: parts falling off a conveyor or shelf, drop tests, packaging. Set the air density to 0 to compare with a vacuum.
